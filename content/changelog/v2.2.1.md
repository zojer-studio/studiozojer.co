There was an issue where aspects in "The week ahead" on the Home page were not reporting the right timestamps.

Rather than showing up at the moment of exactitude, they showed up within orb of it.

The reason is that these heavy calculations were happening on-device, and didn't have enough power to be exactly precise. We've moved these operations to the server.

If you'd like to see transiting aspects to your chart, be sure to add your chart to your vault, via the profile page!
