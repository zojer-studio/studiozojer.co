Last time we said we were shifting focus toward making the planets legible the moment you open the app. This update is a step in that direction.

### What's new

- **Aspects Now** — the Codex now shows current transit aspects within 3° orb, with a live clock. You can tap any aspect to see (or start) your codex entries for it.
- **Moon condition card** on the Today page, showing the current lunar phase and Moon sign at a glance.
- **Find shortcuts** — the "+" capture menu now has "Find charts" and "Find placements" options that jump straight into search with the keyboard ready. Less tapping to get where you're going.
- **Recent configurations** — when you open Codex search, you'll now see your recently viewed placements and aspects. No more starting from a blank page every time.
- **Entry timestamps** — codex entries now show when they were created and last updated.

### Fixes and polish

- Fixed slow app launch caused by ephemeris cache computation blocking the loading screen
- Fixed keyboard appearing on app launch due to a pre-warming race condition
- Fixed Rahu and Ketu aspects not appearing in Codex search results
- Fixed codex overlays not covering the full screen (content from the page underneath was bleeding through at the bottom)
- Improved bottom chrome expand/collapse with slide and fade transitions
- Lunar node fingerprints are now consistent regardless of mean/true node setting
- Fixed issue with automatic house highlighting 

### Where we're headed

The Today page is next. Right now it shows ephemeris rows and the moon card — useful, but static. We want it to become something closer to a personal transit dashboard: aspects approaching your natal chart, ingresses that matter to you, and a sense of what's building over the coming days.

Alongside that, we're continuing to make the Codex faster and easier to navigate. The recent configurations feature is a small example — the goal is for the app to learn your patterns and surface the right things without you having to dig.

More soon. Thanks for sticking with us.
