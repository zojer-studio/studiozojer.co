This release brings two big additions — a proper tarot experience and the first editorial content — plus a handful of refinements. Here's what's new.

## The divination tab

The codex tab has been replaced with a new **Divination** tab — a full tarot experience built around an actual deck. It's meant to feel physical: pick it up, shuffle it, cut it, riffle through the cards, and draw a reading. Cards are drawn from the classic Rider-Waite-Smith deck, reversals and all, and your readings live on your device.

For now it's freeform — draw and lay out cards however you like. Structured spreads are coming (see below).

## Editorial content

Select codex entries now carry **editorial content**, written and curated rather than generated. This is just the start — we'll be fleshing it out over time. Currently, there's a post for each placement in the sky right now - plus a few others.

If you'd like to apply to join the editorial team, send an email to **pageofswrds@zojer.studio**. It might take me a while to get back to you, but I read every one.

## Pro mode

Pro mode can now show the **stepper and tabs at the same time**, for more control on screen at once. Flip it on in Settings.

## Today page

The **home/Today page** has had an update. After talking with a handful of users, it seems we like the calendar-style view the best! Currently, it shows every upcoming astrological event throughout the week. You can click on events to see them in the chart, or take a look at the relevant codex entrie(s). This one is very much in progress — expect this page to keep evolving over the next few releases.

## On the horizon

- Pre-built tarot spreads, with a slot for each card
- Save your spreads and attach them to your astrology journal
- Social posts (I think I figured it out!!)
