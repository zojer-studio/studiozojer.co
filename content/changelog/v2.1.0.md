This update is focused on solidifying the navigational foundation of the app. We've added a few new entry points, polished the chart experience, and started laying down rails for things to come. Here's what's new.

## Today

A new tab to show you what the sky is doing *right now*. **Today** surfaces your active transits at a glance — your launchpad for the day. Select a chart from your vault\* to get started.

\*more on the vault in a minute

## A polished chart tab

The chart tab has been given a fresh coat of paint and a tighter feel overall. We've also added **pro mode** for folks who want more density and more controls on screen — flip it on in your user settings.

## Refreshed display presets

There's a new set of display presets shipping with this release, with refreshed styles we're really happy with. If you want to try them out, head to settings and tap **Reset Presets to Defaults**. (You might want to save your current presets as a backup first!)

## The new chart page

Tapping a chart now opens a dedicated **chart page** with more information at a glance — and quick access to placements and patterns so you can spin up codex entries without breaking your flow.

## The vault

The **vault** is your personal collection of charts. Anything you save lives here — natals, transits, composites, whatever you're working with. It's the source you'll be pulling from when you open Today, the chart tab, or the new chart page. Think of it as the shelf you keep your most-thumbed books on.

## Global search

Search for anything. Looking up *Jupiter conjunct Venus*? Type it in. Global search reaches across your charts, the codex, placements, patterns — the whole app — so you can get where you're going fast.

## Codex tab

The codex is your astrological reference *and* your astrological notebook. This release is also the first step toward social features — eventually you'll be able to publish your codex entries to the Atmosphere for others to read. **Nothing is shared yet** — for now everything stays on your device. We'll be building out the publishing flow over the coming releases.

## Dashboard

The **dashboard** is in-progress, and we want your input. The idea is a customizable surface for the tools you reach for most. Two we're thinking about: a **calendar** view for ongoing transits and aspects, and **planetary hours** for the day. What else would you want there? Let us know.
