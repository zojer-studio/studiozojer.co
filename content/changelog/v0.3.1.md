New ways to interact with your charts.

## New Features

**Fast Time Stepping**
Tap and hold to step through time rapidly without lag.

**Marking Menus**
Tap and hold the plus button to quickly add charts. This gesture will expand to more actions in future updates.

**Orientation Lock**
Keep the chart fixed in place while you explore.

**Planet Selection**
Tap planets to select them - aspect functionality coming soon.
