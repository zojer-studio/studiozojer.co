You can now save and manage your charts.

## New Features

**Saved Charts**
Create, edit, and delete charts. Your data persists between sessions.

**Multi-Ring Display**
The chart wheel can hold up to three charts at once. Tap and drag to reorder them or remove from view.

**Tags & Filtering**
Early tagging system to help organize your charts.
