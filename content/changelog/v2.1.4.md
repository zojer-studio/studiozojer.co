A round of polish — small refinements across display, charts, and a couple of fixes.

**Charts**

**Fixes**
- Reworked the account deletion flow to follow atproto standards
- Fixed recent patterns not showing up in your search history

**Presets**
- Updated the default display presets — head to Settings and tap **Reset Presets to Defaults** to pick them up
